# ADR-0015: Domain contract and affirmative product evaluation

- **Status**: **ratified by the owner on 2026-09-26; implemented and accepted by the owner on 2026-09-27**. ## Decision

A second evaluator profile, `product-evidence-v1`, is added on top of the pack JSON `0.1` format of ADR-0013. It is the profile that may emit affirmative product states — `affected`, `fixed` or `not_affected` — alongside `under_investigation`, using three families of product evidence facts carried in bundle schema `0.2`.

1. **Profile, not a new engine.** `evidence-readiness-v1` keeps its four predicates, requirements, emissions, admission and reasons unchanged. The pair `(pack schema_version, profile)` identifies the contract: `("0.1", "product-evidence-v1")` is not interchangeable with `("0.1", "evidence-readiness-v1")`. A reader that only knows the earlier profile rejects the new one with `unsupported_pack`; it never reinterprets or silently downgrades it.
2. **Three families of scalar facts.** Product evidence is carried as evidence items whose `type` follows the closed grammar of ADR-0016: `product_v1.mapping.*` (the explicit correspondence between the scanner finding and a vendor product/release/platform/component), `product_v1.artifact.*` (explicit inspection of the exact build, bound to the observed digest and platform) and `product_v1.vendor.*` (explicit vendor support for the exact build and vulnerability). The catalogue is the 59 names fixed by ADR-0016; no wildcard, no free-form extension. Each family requires its own confidence (`derived` for mapping, `observed` for artifact and vendor), its own exact field set and its own source obligations. The artifact family carries the digest-class claim `product_v1.artifact.digest_kind=platform_manifest`: it is a producer-supported statement, it must match an independently accredited `normalized_digest`, it is never inferred from the hash algorithm, the OS or the architecture, and an index digest or an ambiguous class does not satisfy it. It adds no wire field and never promotes an index digest into `normalized_digest`.
3. **Exactly three proof variants, not interchangeable.** `vulnerable_build` supports `affected`; `fixed_build` supports `fixed`; `code_excluded_build` supports `not_affected`. A patch that fixes vulnerable code belongs to `fixed_build` and cannot be renamed as `not_affected`. For `not_affected`, only demonstrated exclusion of all vulnerable code for that component and vulnerability in an exact build qualifies; an empty inventory, a negative search or a complete SBOM export is not proof.
4. **Five new predicates, one terminal per affirmative rule.** `redhat_product_mapped`, `redhat_artifact_bound`, `redhat_build_affected`, `redhat_build_fixed` and `redhat_build_code_excluded`, each with exact parameters and required operands. An affirmative rule declares the two base requirements plus nine additional ones explicitly — omission is `invalid_pack`, never silently derived from the terminal predicate. `emit` and its terminal predicate must correspond: `affected`→`redhat_build_affected`, `fixed`→`redhat_build_fixed`, `not_affected`→`redhat_build_code_excluded`.
5. **Explicit context and validity.** The new profile adds an internal `DomainContext` (maximum evidence age, explicit source pins with a canonical order) that is not part of the bundle and is not required by the earlier profile. Every observation used must satisfy `observed_at <= evaluated_at` and `evaluated_at - observed_at < maximum_evidence_age_seconds`: equality with the limit expires. An expired pack is an error with no result; missing, future, expired or unpinned evidence produces an inconclusive result with reasons and references.
6. **Conflict is blocked, not resolved.** When valid proofs support two or three different affirmative states, the result is `under_investigation` with **all** supported branches preserved and visible, together with their references. There is no numeric priority, no fixed hierarchy, no first/last rule, no majority and no "newest source wins": those order declarations, they do not prove which is true. Duplicating a rule or proof adds no authority.
7. **Warnings can block every affirmative emission.** All provenance warnings and all warnings of the target's items are inspected, even when no rule selects that item. A `contradictory` warning, or one whose code is unknown, blocks **all** affirmative emissions of this profile — `affected`, `fixed` and `not_affected` — with no pack-side exception. For `not_affected` this preserves the wire rule of ADR-0006 §8 unchanged; for `affected` and `fixed` it is an additional obligation of this profile, not a change to the wire validator or its versioned policy. A known `informational` warning neither blocks on its own nor compensates for missing proof, and no time-to-live, failure or identity change is inferred from free-text messages.
8. **Version and profile mismatch is rejected without a result.** The new profile requires bundle `0.2`. A valid `0.1` bundle is not admitted for this profile and is rejected with zero result using the existing `invalid_pack` code, in the pack schema/profile phase and before anti-downgrade, validity and ruleset binding. It is not reclassified as malformed: it is valid for its own contract and merely incompatible with this profile.

## Context

The initial evaluator profile (ADR-0013) only emits `under_investigation` and `not_assessed`. Its checks establish presence, equality or linkage of facts, but they do not include product mapping, vendor proof or any implementable conflict policy between affirmative states. A finding's `fix_status`, a package version or the absence of a row cannot demonstrate affection or safety, and a package-presence match is not proof of what a specific deployed build contains.

Closing that gap needs facts the earlier vocabulary cannot express, and it needs them bound to an exact build. The tempting shortcuts — letting a pack declare the state, comparing upstream versions, treating a missing result as absence, or letting a scanner label decide — were all rejected: each converts unverified input into technical authority.

A first draft of this contract proposed carrying the new vocabulary without a schema version change, on the grounds that the evidence item's `type` is an open string. An independent review found that argument lacked normative authority: the ratified records state that the vocabulary is versioned with the schema, that new types or vocabulary are a wire change requiring their own record and a version bump, and that representing the digest class requires exactly that. The owner stopped that route and had the versioning question decided separately; the result is the ratified ADR-0016, which fixes the minor bump to bundle `0.2`. This record is the re-opening delta that inherits that contract.

## Options considered

| Question | Options | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| Engine scope | New profile with exact-build proofs and conflict blocking / general EVR-range and OVAL interpreter / state declared by the pack or derived from scanner fields / keep the inconclusive profile only | New profile |
| Not-affected route | Code exclusion in an exact build only / also component absence, negative reachability, uncontrollable input, inline mitigation | Code exclusion only |
| Conflict | Block to `under_investigation` keeping all supported branches / numeric priority / fixed hierarchy / newest source / majority | Block and preserve |
| Requirements in a pack | Declared explicitly by the rule / derived silently from the terminal predicate | Declared explicitly |
| Coverage claim | Producer assertion with source obligations / treated as material sufficiency proven by the engine | Producer assertion |
| Versioning | Minor bump to `0.2` with the catalogue (chosen, via ADR-0016) / no bump with a separate catalogue (stopped) / structural wire field for the digest class / drop the digest-class claim / keep `0.1` and suspend the affirmative scope | Minor bump |

A general EVR/OVAL interpreter would widen coverage but requires RPM semantics, conditions, dependencies and source adaptation that are not accredited; it is not needed for the exact-build control. Letting the pack declare the state would make a text file the source of technical authority. A structural field for the digest class adds keys, projection and validation with no demonstrated need, and a second representation of the same claim. Removing the digest-class claim does not avoid the bump, because the rest of the vocabulary still requires it.

## Why this was the best at the time

- It closes the gap where it lives: the affirmative states become provable from accredited facts bound to an exact build, digest and platform, while the conservative profile and the bundle structure stay untouched.
- Every choice fails closed in the direction that matters: incomplete evidence never becomes `not_affected`, conflicting proofs never resolve by convenience, a coverage label is never treated as material sufficiency, and an incompatible version is rejected rather than interpreted.
- It reuses ratified mechanisms instead of adding vocabulary: the bundle versioning of ADR-0006, the digest-class requirement of ADR-0010, the evidence-item mapping and value-hash preimage of ADR-0012, and the admission, trace and reference model of ADR-0013.
- The conflict policy is honest about what the tool knows: two valid but incompatible proofs are a reason to investigate, not a reason to pick the more convenient one.
- The scope is deliberately synthetic at first. A limited profile with explicit synthetic proofs can be implemented and tested now, with the absence of real producers declared as a known limit instead of hidden.

## Consequences and limits

- A domain conclusion is scoped to the component of the finding, its vulnerability, UID, container class/name, observed digest and accredited platform. It never claims that the whole workload is free of the vulnerability.
- Bundle `0.2` is required by this profile, and bundle `0.2` is not implemented. The catalogue and the domain contract must be reconciled before `0.2` is frozen; the implementation, its tests and the synthetic fixtures are separate work.
- **Not guaranteed**: authenticity of any source, correctness or completeness of a producer, currency of a source or of the cluster, coverage of EVR ranges or OVAL semantics, immutability of a running filesystem, universal absence of vulnerability, exploitability, workload security, human approval, durable persistence, or real Red Hat/OpenShift compatibility.
- `fixed` remains distinct from `not_affected`. The three decision layers stay separate: `product_status` is the only layer this profile fills; `exploitability` is always `not_assessed` and there is never a `risk_decision`, an exception recommendation or an approval. Severity, affection, exploitability and the human risk decision remain different questions. `EXCEPCIONABLE` is never a technical state.
- The `coverage=complete` of a domain record is a producer assertion about a bounded scope, not a proof of exhaustiveness that the engine verifies.
- Append-only conservation of previous results is an obligation of future integration, not a storage capability delivered by the evaluator. Without receiving external changes, the offline engine discovers neither invalidations nor cluster currency.

## Revisit if

A real producer and a reviewable corpus appear, which must demonstrate source adequacy, extraction and scope before any real use; EVR ranges, OVAL semantics, other vendors or several installations of the same component are needed; sources cannot accredit a platform manifest, product/platform identity or the exclusion of all vulnerable code required by a variant; observing a mutable filesystem or runtime probing is required (it is not resolved by informally widening permissions); method or bundle aggregation, another proof category, state or justification is needed; a measured corpus shows the budgets, field limits or pins are insufficient; a public evaluation wire, VEX export, signing, source distribution, protected pin/history storage, a protected clock or persisted invalidation is required; or a wire reader is shown to need a change in its validation or projection to transport these facts. Any of these reopens the decision with a new record; none of them authorizes inventing fields, changing the wire or editing an accepted record in silence.

## Normative detail

This record fixes the domain contract: the profile, the three evidence families, the proof variants, the predicates, the context and validity rules, the conflict policy and the version/profile boundary. The exact catalogue, grammar and versioning of the vocabulary live in the ratified ADR-0016; the pack admission, traces and references come from ADR-0013. Verification of the implementation, the synthetic fixtures and the public specification of bundle `0.2` are separate steps, and this record does not complete them.
